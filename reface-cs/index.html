EDITOR

QUICK SETUP

  1. Plug the Reface CS in with USB and switch it on.
  2. Use Chrome, Edge or Brave and allow MIDI access, including SysEx. The editor connects by itself; otherwise press Connect (choose the Reface CS as MIDI In and Out).
  3. Move a slider on the synth: its dashed shadow shows where the hardware control sits.

IN BRIEF

  • Library: 128 slots on 4 pages, stored in this browser (Export to back up; drop a .json or .syx to import). Double-click a slot to name it, right-click for a colour, drag to reorder (Alt/Option copies). Select a slot and press S to save into it, C or Delete to clear. Arrow keys walk the library; Shift+click or Shift+arrows selects a block of the grid (like a rectangle between two slots), Ctrl/Cmd+click picks slots one by one, Ctrl/Cmd+A selects the page. Drag any selected slot to move the whole selection (Alt/Option copies), and the landing slots preview as you hover. With several selected, Random re-rolls each patch on its own and Clear removes them all. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes, add Shift to redo.
  • Morph: press MORPH, click a patch for A and another for B, then slide to blend. Selectors switch at the halfway point.
  • Synth: scroll over a fader to move it, double-click to return it to the hardware position. The gear holds master tune, transpose, bend range and MIDI Control (needed for the editor to follow the synth’s own sliders).
